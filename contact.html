Opportunities

Our lab pursues interdisciplinary research in plant, soil, and microbial ecology. We are committed to building a diverse and inclusive community of scholars. Please email me if you are interested in our research activities.

  • Postdocs: If your research interests align with those of the lab, I would be thrilled to initiate new collaborations with postdoctoral researchers. Please send me an email with your CV and a statement of research interests describing the type of projects you would like to pursue.
  • Graduate students: I am actively recruiting highly motivated, independent, and passionate students through the Integrative Biology Department. I expect graduate students to take advantage of the resources provided by the University of South Florida and to pursue their own funding opportunities. If you are interested in joining the lab, please send me an email with your CV and a brief statement describing why you are interested in my lab and outlining your previous research experience.
  • Undergraduate students: Curious and highly motivated undergraduate students are welcome to join the lab. If you are interested, please send me an email with your interests and a short statement describing why you would like to join the lab.
